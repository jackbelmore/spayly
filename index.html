spayly · controls

Drag
orbit the view · right-drag or shift-drag pans · wheel zooms (log scale)
W A S D
pan the view; hold shift to move faster. Panning keeps following whatever you are following.
Q · E
swing the camera left and right around what it is looking at
Click / double-click
select a body / fly to it — on the body itself or on its name in the view
Space, [ ]
pause · slower · faster
R
rewind time (gravity is time-reversible; impacts are undone from the report card)
Z
erase mode: drag over the view to delete asteroids and fragments, wheel resizes the brush
X
asteroid mode: click a body to fire from the camera with auto-aim, or drag on empty space to aim by hand. Rocks you fire are ordinary bodies — shoot one at another and they pull on each other, swing around each other, and collide.
F · T · 1–9, 0
fly above the selection · tabletop view · select Sun, Mercury … Neptune, Pluto
Sliders
change any body's mass or radius live. The Sun's luminosity and colour follow its mass; watch Earth freeze or scorch.
Escape
back to explore mode, cancel aiming, close panels

Physics: full mutual N-body gravity (symplectic leapfrog, adaptive substeps with swept-sphere contact detection), IAU rotation poles, Standish planetary ephemeris, JPL satellite and small-body elements, Collins–Melosh–Marcus impact effects, Benz–Asphaug disruption thresholds, momentum-conserving merges and fragmentation. Visual scale never changes the physics.
